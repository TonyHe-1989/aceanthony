Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information the iOS game Ace & Anthony (“the game”, “we”) handles, why, and the choices you have. The game is made by an independent developer. Questions: dongshenghe1989@gmail.com.

1. No account; progress lives on your device and your own iCloud

The game has no sign-up or login. Your progress, stars, in-game currency and energy are saved on your device and, if you are signed in to iCloud, in your private iCloud storage (Apple CloudKit) so they sync across your devices. This data stays in your own Apple account; we cannot see or access it. Settings such as volume stay on the device. Deleting the game removes the device copy; you can remove the iCloud copy in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage.

2. Feedback you choose to send

If you use Settings → Feedback, we receive:

  • the message you write;
  • your email address, only if you enter it (optional);
  • basic diagnostics attached automatically: app version, device model, iOS version, language, and game progress (story level, total stars, highest tower floor).

The form is submitted to Google Forms and stored in a Google Sheet owned by the developer (Google acts as our service provider; see Google’s Privacy Policy). If you contact us by email instead, we receive your email address and message.

We use feedback only to answer you and to fix or improve the game. We do not sell it or use it for advertising. We delete feedback when it is no longer needed, or earlier at your request.

3. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details; we only receive confirmation from Apple that a purchase is valid so the game can deliver it.

4. Rewarded ads

The game offers optional video ads you can choose to watch for rewards. Ads are served by Google AdMob and its mediation partner ad networks. To show and measure ads, they may collect device and usage information such as IP address, device model, OS version, the app-specific vendor identifier, and ad interactions. The Apple advertising identifier (IDFA) is used only if you allow it in the App Tracking Transparency prompt; you can change this anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), we ask for your consent first, and you can change your choice anytime in the game’s Settings → Ad Privacy Settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Monthly Pass holders do not see ads.

5. Children

The game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, and to object to or restrict its use. To make a request, email us; we will respond within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

隐私政策

Ace & Anthony · 生效日期：2026 年 10 月 13 日

本政策说明 iOS 游戏 Ace & Anthony（以下称“本游戏”“我们”）会处理哪些信息、为什么处理，以及你可以做的选择。本游戏由独立开发者制作。如有疑问请联系：dongshenghe1989@gmail.com。

1. 无需账号，进度保存在你的设备和你自己的 iCloud 中

本游戏没有注册或登录。你的游戏进度、星数、游戏内货币和体力保存在你的设备上；如果你登录了 iCloud，还会保存在你私人的 iCloud 空间（Apple CloudKit）中，以便在你的多台设备间同步。这些数据只在你自己的 Apple 账户里，我们无法查看或访问。音量等设置只保存在本机。删除游戏会删除设备上的副本；iCloud 中的副本可在 iOS「设置 → 你的姓名 → iCloud → 管理储存空间」中删除。

2. 你主动提交的反馈

当你使用「设置 → 问题反馈」时，我们会收到：

  • 你填写的反馈内容；
  • 你的邮箱地址（仅当你选择填写时）；
  • 自动附带的基础诊断信息：App 版本、设备型号、iOS 版本、语言，以及游戏进度（剧情关卡、总星数、幻时之境最高层）。

表单提交至 Google 表单，并保存在开发者自己的 Google 表格中（Google 作为我们的服务提供方，参见 Google 隐私权政策）。如果你改用邮件联系我们，我们会收到你的邮箱地址和邮件内容。

反馈仅用于回复你以及修复、改进游戏，我们不会出售，也不会用于广告。反馈在不再需要时删除；你也可以随时要求我们提前删除。

3. 购买

游戏内购买由 Apple 处理。我们看不到你的支付信息，只会收到 Apple 对购买有效性的确认，以便在游戏中发放商品。

4. 激励广告

本游戏提供可选的视频广告，你可以自愿观看以获得奖励。广告由 Google AdMob 及其聚合合作广告网络提供。为了展示和统计广告，它们可能收集设备与使用信息，例如 IP 地址、设备型号、系统版本、App 专属的供应商标识符以及广告互动情况。仅当你在「App 跟踪透明度」弹窗中允许时，才会使用 Apple 广告标识符（IDFA）；你可以随时在 iOS「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 跟踪」中更改。在法律要求的地区（如欧洲经济区、英国），我们会先征求你的同意，你也可以随时在游戏「设置 → 广告隐私设置」中修改。详见 Google 如何使用来自合作应用的信息。月卡用户不会看到广告。

5. 儿童

本游戏面向一般大众，并非专为 13 岁以下儿童设计。我们不会在知情的情况下收集儿童的个人信息。如果你认为有儿童向我们提交了个人信息，请联系我们，我们会将其删除。

6. 你的权利

根据你所在地区的法律（例如欧盟 / 英国 GDPR），你可能有权访问、更正或删除我们持有的你的个人信息，并有权反对或限制其使用。如需行使上述权利，请发邮件给我们，我们会在 30 天内回复。你也可以向当地的数据保护机构投诉。

7. 变更

如本政策有所变更，我们会更新本页面及上方的生效日期。
